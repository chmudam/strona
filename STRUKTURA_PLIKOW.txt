ROUTING (React Router DOM)
================================================================

/kluby?voivodeship=mazowieckie → ClubsListPage (filtrowane)
/klub/1                     → ClubDetailsPage
/klub/:id                   → ClubDetailsPage
/sparingpartnerzy           → SparingPartnersPage
/sparingpartner/:id         → (możliwe rozszerzenie)
/turnieje                   → TournamentsPage
/o-nas                      → AboutPage
/login                      → LoginPage
/register                   → LoginPage
